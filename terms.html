3. Payment Terms

We accept prepaid payments via Razorpay (UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, RuPay/Visa/MasterCard Debit/Credit Cards, Net Banking) as well as Cash on Delivery (COD) for serviceable Indian postal codes.

For prepaid orders, production and dispatch begin immediately upon payment confirmation. For COD orders, a customer verification check may be conducted via WhatsApp or phone before shipping.

4. Shipping & Delivery Across India

  • Dispatch Window: Standard catalog pieces are carefully packed and dispatched within 24 to 48 business hours.
  • Transit Duration: Delivery typically takes between 3 to 7 business days depending on your Indian state and courier reach.
  • Free Delivery Threshold: Orders totaling ₹499 or more qualify for FREE pan-India delivery. A nominal shipping fee of ₹49 applies to orders under ₹499.
  • Gift-Ready Packaging: All orders are dispatched in reinforced crush-proof corrugated boxes to prevent delicate wire petals from bending during transit.

Because pipe-cleaner floral creations are delicate handmade items, general change-of-mind returns are not accepted once delivered. However, we stand 100% behind our craft:

  • If your piece arrives broken, damaged, or defective due to transit, you may submit a return or replacement request within 3 days of delivery.
  • To ensure fair verification, please share a quick unboxing video or clear photographs showing the damaged piece and parcel label via WhatsApp or email at hello@raycrafting.co.
  • Upon verification, we will promptly dispatch a fresh replacement free of charge or initiate a full refund to your original payment method.
