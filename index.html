Good to
know.

A few small answers for an easy, thoughtful shopping experience.

How are Raycrafting pieces made?

Each piece is shaped by hand from pipe cleaners, then checked and packed with care. Small variations are part of what makes every piece yours.

Can I request a custom colour or gift?

Yes. Tell the studio what you have in mind through the custom-order link and we’ll work out the colours, size, and timing together.

Where can I find my order after checkout?

Open the profile icon in the top-right corner and choose My orders. You can also contact the studio if you need help.
