Craftsmanship & Care Guide

Every single bloom and keepsake is sculpted with patience and love. Here is everything you need to know about how our pieces are made, how they arrive, and how to keep them vibrant forever.

✳

Hand-Twisted Pipe-Cleaner Blooms

Unlike real cut flowers that wilt within days, Raycrafting creations are everlasting botanical sculptures crafted entirely from velvety chenille florist stems and wire.

  • No Water Needed: Never spray or submerge pipe-cleaner blooms in water.
  • Flexible Shaping: Chenille wire stems can be gently bent and reshaped to suit your favorite vase or desk display.
  • Gentle Dusting: Keep them looking vibrant with a gentle sweep from a soft makeup brush, clean paintbrush, or a light hairdryer burst on cool mode.
✺

Illuminated Lamps & Ambient Lights

Our bestselling lotus blossom lamps and golden lily ambient pieces feature concealed warm LED micro fairy lights for a tranquil bedside or desk glow.

  • Warm-Tone Micro LEDs: Designed to stay cool to the touch even after hours of gentle ambient illumination.
  • Replaceable Mini Cells: Powered by standard, easily replaceable button batteries housed cleanly underneath the craft moss base.
  • Indoor Night Light: Perfect as a peaceful nightstand accent, festive Diwali display, or mindful work-from-home centerpiece.
✦

Handcrafted Clay & Keepsake Boxes

From whimsical clay fridge magnets to our signature handcrafted celebration cake boxes, each piece is individually sculpted, baked, and hand-detailed.

  • Secret Photo Compartments: Custom cake boxes can be personalized with hidden customer polaroid prints nestled under the lid.
  • Strong Ceramic Magnets: Our dessert and toast clay magnets feature high-strength backing that firmly holds notes and recipes to your fridge.
  • Care: Avoid dropping on hard stone floors. Clean with a dry microfiber cloth if needed.
✓

Crush-Proof Packaging & Gifting

We know how important your gifts are. Every Raycrafting piece is packed gift-ready so you can send surprises directly to someone you love.

  • Sturdy Protective Boxes: Each floral bouquet and lamp is cocooned in tissue paper and secured inside a heavy-duty outer carton.
  • Free Handwritten Gift Notes: You can add a heartfelt personalized message at checkout, and we’ll handwrite it with love.
  • Pan-India Shipping: Fast 24-48 hour dispatch with tracked courier delivery across all Indian pin codes (Free on orders over ₹499).
